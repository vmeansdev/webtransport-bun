## C. Question (b): is every R2 finding genuinely closed?

What remains open is not a storm but a hole: a subscriber whose open has not completed or failed (`.catch` at `server.ts:1052-1056` resets `deliveryRequested`) answers would-block to the context, and the amendment says only "handled by the per-subscriber queue as today" — there is no such handling today (a context is not a `QueuedDelivery`; the relay's only retry queue for a non-delivery frame is `controlQueue`, which D2 reserves for control).
