# WebSocket vs WebTransport — R2–R8 Parent Plan

> **For agentic workers:** this plan is referenced by the
> `2026-08-28-ws-wt-real-number.md` plan as the next stage after R1 closes.
> Execute only after R1 is fully green and the R1 trust boundary
> admits the official CLIs.

**Goal:** Define the seven remaining rounds (R2–R8) that take the
WebSocket vs WebTransport comparison campaign from a closed R1 trust
boundary to a reusable, reproducible, auditable measurement campaign.
Each round is a discrete, scoped, per-phase-green delivery; rounds
R1–R8 together close the design spec at
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-ws-wt-scenario-comparison-design.md`.

**Architecture:** Seven rounds, each one a single end-to-end pipeline
stage. R1 closes the trust boundary. R2 authors the executor. R3
authors the ledger (already in the tree; the round is to formalize
the contract). R4 authors the controller (already in the tree; the
round is to formalize the orchestration contract). R5 authors the
runner (the per-cell execution driver that combines executor + ledger
+ controller). R6 authors the verifier (already in the tree; the
round is to formalize the evidence-status contract). R7 authors the
reporter (already in the tree; the round is to formalize the
comparison-summary contract). R8 authors the evidence pack (the
single artifact directory that combines runner output, verifier
verdicts, and reporter rows).

**Tech stack:** Same as the parent plan. Bun 1.3.14+ native WebSocket
APIs, webtransport-bun native addon, TypeScript, Bun test, Node-API
napi-rs, Linux `ip`/`tc`/`ss`, macOS `route`/`ifconfig`, SSH/SCP,
JSON + Markdown evidence.

**Design source:**
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-22-ws-wt-scenario-comparison-design.md`
and the closed R1 contract at `tools/compare/output-policy.ts` and
`tools/compare/check-official-io.ts`.

**Starting source:** the post-Phase-4 HEAD of
`codex/ws-scenario-comparison` after the real-number plan lands.

**Relationship to R1:** R1 is the trust boundary. R2–R8 are the
mechanics inside that boundary. Every R2–R8 round inherits the
R1 invariants: official-I/O allowlist (six TypeScript classes plus
`cliEntryTs`), forbidden imports/calls, frozen-test F4 thread-through,
frozen-R1-bundle rule, native llvm-cov floors, per-phase gate. The
R1 outputs (supervisor-observed per-host digests, two-host toolchain /
capability / lock / manifest sets) are the **inputs** to R2–R8.

## R2 — Executor

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/executor.ts` (new file; classified as
`roleChildTs` per the official-I-O allowlist).

**Inputs:** a `ScenarioCell` (name + parameters + delivery mode) and
a `Transport` (ws | wt). Per-host digests (toolchain / capability /
lock / manifest) from the supervisor via `supervisor-client.ts`.

**Outputs:** a `MeasuredLeg` (per-session loopUtilization, round-trips,
samples, ledger). The execution runs through the adapter
(`adapters/ws.ts` or `adapters/wt.ts`) with the controller
(`bin/compare-controller.ts`) on the bench host.

**Failure modes:**
- Transport adapter raises: the leg is sealed with
  `kind: "transport-failed"` and a typed error code.
- Server unreachable: the leg is sealed with
  `kind: "server-unreachable"` and a typed error code.
- Server accepts but produces no samples within the bounded window:
  the leg is sealed with `kind: "no-traffic"` and a typed error code.

**Relationship to R1:** R2 reads the supervisor-observed digests; R2
never publishes its own toolchain / capability / lock / manifest. R2's
leg is sealed under the per-host digests R1 admitted.

**Dependencies:** the closed R1 trust boundary; the per-scenario
executors defined in Phase 2.1 of the real-number plan; the
controller defined in Phase 3 of the real-number plan.

---

## R3 — Ledger

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/ledger.ts` (new file; the existing
`scenarios/*.ts` files are the per-scenario leg factories, not the
ledger itself).

**Inputs:** a `MeasuredLeg` from R2 and a `ScenarioCell`.

**Outputs:** a `Ledger` — the sequence of round-trips behind the
samples, in the order they were recorded. The ledger is what makes
the measurement reproducible: every sample has a corresponding
ledger entry, and every ledger entry has a corresponding sample.

**Failure modes:**
- Sample count and ledger length disagree: the artifact is rejected
  at `buildRunArtifact` (artifact-builder.ts) with
  `LEDGER_LENGTH_MISMATCH`.
- Ledger entry references a transport the cell does not declare: the
  artifact is rejected with `LEDGER_TRANSPORT_MISMATCH`.
- Ledger entry's recorded round-trip time is negative or
  non-finite: the artifact is rejected with
  `LEDGER_TIME_INVALID`.

**Relationship to R1:** R3 reads the per-host digests R1 admitted
and stamps them on every ledger entry. The ledger is the durable
record of what the executor actually did; the digests are the
durable record of what the executor did it on.

**Dependencies:** R2 (executor); the existing `scenarios/*.ts` files.

---

## R4 — Controller

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/bin/compare-controller.ts` (already in
the tree from the real-number plan; classified as `controllerOnlyTs`).

**Inputs:** a `RunSpec` (cell list, repetition count, rig endpoints)
and the supervisor's per-host digests.

**Outputs:** orchestration across the two hosts: SSH, SCP, route
verify, netem apply/restore, balanced arm execution, evidence pack
write.

**Failure modes:**
- Route verify fails (no direct cable): the controller aborts with
  `RIG_ROUTE_MISSING` and writes a deviation record.
- Netem apply fails (kernel rejects the qdisc): the controller
  aborts with `RIG_NETEM_APPLY_FAILED` and restores best-effort.
- Server start fails (port in use, certificate mismatch): the
  controller aborts with `RIG_SERVER_START_FAILED` and tears down
  any partial state.
- Evidence write fails (path outside `OFFICIAL_COMPARISON_OUTPUT_ROOT`):
  the controller aborts with `RIG_EVIDENCE_PATH_INVALID`.

**Relationship to R1:** R4 inherits the R1 official-I/O allowlist.
The controller's evidence writes go to the R1-mandated root
(`.release-evidence/transport-comparison/<candidate>/<campaignId>/<run-id>/`).
R4 does not import the supervisor directly; the supervisor is
already running on the controller host as a separate process, and
the controller reads its output via the `supervisor-client.ts` API.

**Dependencies:** R1 (trust boundary), R2 (executor), R3 (ledger).

## R5 — Runner

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/run-campaign.ts` (already in the tree;
classified as `officialRoots`).

**Inputs:** a `CampaignSpec` (cell list, repetition count, expected
outcomes) and the supervisor's per-host digests.

**Outputs:** a `RunArtifact` — the sealed composition of N
`MeasuredArmArtifact` (one per arm per cell) and the per-host
digests R1 admitted. Sealing is `sealRunArtifact` from
`tools/compare/evidence.ts`.

**Failure modes:**
- Cell declares a leg the executor cannot produce: the run skips
  the cell with a typed `LEG_PLAN_UNDEFINED` and continues.
- Cell declares a leg the ledger cannot match: the run aborts with
  `LEDGER_LENGTH_MISMATCH` (the existing validator anchor at
  `verify-artifact.ts:2647`).
- Sealing fails (digest mismatch): the run aborts with
  `SEAL_DIGEST_MISMATCH` and the artifact is not written.

**Relationship to R1:** R5 is the R1 boundary's official runner.
Every artifact R5 produces carries the R1-mandated per-host digests
and is verified against the R1 binding checks (F4 pattern from
commit `2fb90c13`). R5 is the only path that can write to
`OFFICIAL_COMPARISON_OUTPUT_ROOT`; the boundary is enforced at
`output-policy.ts:336` and `check-official-io.ts:3911`.

**Dependencies:** R1 (trust boundary), R2 (executor), R3 (ledger),
R4 (controller).

---

## R6 — Verifier

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/verify-artifact.ts` (already in the
tree; classified as `officialRoots`).

**Inputs:** a sealed `RunArtifact` and a `TrustContext` (per-host
digests R1 admitted).

**Outputs:** a `Verification` — `evidenceStatus: "PASS" | "FAIL"`,
list of typed rejection codes, list of typed acceptance reasons.

**Failure modes:**
- Rejection codes are typed: `LEDGER_LENGTH_MISMATCH`,
  `LEDGER_TRANSPORT_MISMATCH`, `LEDGER_TIME_INVALID`,
  `SEAL_DIGEST_MISMATCH`, `TOOLCHAIN_SUPERVISOR_MISSING`,
  `TOOLCHAIN_SUPERVISOR_MISMATCH` (and the parallel `*_SUPERVISOR_*`
  codes from Phase 1 of the real-number plan), `LOOP_UTILIZATION_MISSING`,
  `LOOP_UTILIZATION_INVALID`, `BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, `RATE_LIMITED`.
- Every typed rejection has a single canonical definition; no
  rejection is allowed to mean two things.

**Relationship to R1:** R6 is the R1 boundary's official verifier.
Every verdict R6 emits is reproducible: the same artifact + the same
`TrustContext` produces the same `Verification`. R6 is read-only; it
never writes to the official output root.

**Dependencies:** R1 (trust boundary), R5 (runner).

---

## R7 — Reporter

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/render-report.ts` (already in the tree;
classified as `officialRoots`).

**Inputs:** a list of sealed `RunArtifact` (one per repetition per
cell) and the per-host digests R1 admitted.

**Outputs:** a `ComparisonSummary` (in-memory) and a Markdown report
(written under `OFFICIAL_COMPARISON_OUTPUT_ROOT` by R5, not R7).
The report has the columns: scenario, transport, sample count,
per-session loopUtilization, server-aggregate loopUtilization,
saturation caveat (if any), per-arm mean / p50 / p99, cross-arm delta,
and the typed rejection codes (if any).

**Failure modes:**
- The renderer refuses to publish a comparison where the
  saturation caveat fires on more than 30% of rows; the report is
  emitted with a `REPORT_SATURATION_GUARD_TRIPPED` banner and the
  operator must decide whether to publish.
- The renderer refuses to publish a comparison where any
  per-arm row lacks loopUtilization on either perSession or
  serverAggregate; the report is emitted with
  `REPORT_LOOP_UTILIZATION_MISSING` and a list of the missing rows.

**Relationship to R1:** R7 reads the R1-admitted digests and the
R1-validated artifacts. R7 never invents a number; every cell in
the report corresponds to a row in a sealed artifact.

**Dependencies:** R1, R5 (runner), R6 (verifier).

---

## R8 — Evidence pack

**Entry point:** `tools/compare/evidence-pack.ts` (new file; classified
as `roleChildTs`).

**Inputs:** a `CampaignId`, a list of sealed `RunArtifact`, the
verifier verdicts, the reporter output, and the per-host digests R1
admitted.

**Outputs:** a single self-contained directory at
`OFFICIAL_COMPARISON_OUTPUT_ROOT/<candidate>/<campaignId>/<run-id>/`
containing:
- `manifest.json` — the campaign ID, candidate ID, the per-host
  digests (toolchain / capability / lock / manifest), the list of
  artifacts in the pack, the SHA-256 of each.
- `artifacts/<run-id>.json` — each sealed `RunArtifact`.
- `verdicts/<run-id>.json` — each verifier verdict.
- `report.md` — the reporter output.
- `git.json` — the campaign's source HEAD, the plan file SHA-256,
  the approval record path.
- `replay.sh` — a self-contained script that, given a Mac↔Linux rig
  and the supervisor binary, reproduces the campaign.

**Failure modes:**
- Manifest SHA-256 does not match the artifact: the pack is rejected
  with `PACK_DIGEST_MISMATCH`.
- Replay script references a binary the rig does not have: the
  replay aborts with `REPLAY_BINARY_MISSING` (the script is
  required to verify the binaries up-front, not at replay time).

**Relationship to R1:** R8 is the durable, reproducible, auditable
output of the entire campaign. The R1 trust boundary is what makes
R8 possible: every artifact in the pack carries the per-host digests
R1 admitted, and the verifier verdicts R1 enforces are the
verdicts R8 records. Without R1, R8 is a list of numbers; with R1,
R8 is a measurement.

**Dependencies:** R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7.

## Risk register

- **R1 invariants must survive R2–R8.** Every round inherits the R1
  official-I/O allowlist, the forbidden imports/calls, the frozen
  test F4 thread-through, the frozen-R1-bundle rule, and the native
  llvm-cov floors. A round that breaks an R1 invariant does not
  land; the round's author must re-thread the F4 pattern.
- **Evidence-pack reproducibility.** R8's `replay.sh` must work on
  a fresh rig with no human intervention. The script must verify
  the binaries up-front, not at replay time, and must abort
  cleanly on any missing binary.
- **Saturation guard.** R7's 30% saturation-trip guard is a
  default; the threshold is a deliverable design choice that
  matches the real-number plan's fixed 0.3 threshold. Changing
  the threshold requires a documented commit and a critic pass.
- **WASM scope leak.** All eight rounds are native-only. WASM stays
  inside `crates/wasm/`; the comparison campaign does not import
  any WASM module.
